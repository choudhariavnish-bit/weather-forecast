Weather of India

Understanding Indian Weather

India experiences a wide range of weather conditions because of its large geographical area, varied terrain, the Himalayas, surrounding seas and seasonal monsoon circulation.

Southwest Monsoon

From around June to September, the southwest monsoon supplies a major share of annual rainfall across many parts of India.

Winter

Winter conditions vary substantially from the Himalayan region to peninsular and coastal India.

Summer

Pre-monsoon temperatures can become very high in inland and northern regions, while coastal areas are moderated by the sea.

Regional Diversity

Rainfall and temperature patterns differ greatly between the Himalayas, plains, deserts, plateaus and coastal regions.
