About Indian Weather

India has diverse weather and climate patterns shaped by its geography, the Himalayas, surrounding seas and seasonal monsoon circulation.

The southwest monsoon is particularly important for rainfall over large parts of the country. Weather conditions can vary considerably between northern, central, eastern, western, southern and Himalayan regions.
